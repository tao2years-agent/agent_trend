L7@P0: ABSTRACT
L8@P0: We present the results of an investigation into the nature
L9@P0: of the information needs of software developers who work
L10@P0: in projects that are part of larger ecosystems. In an openquestion survey we asked framework and library developers
L11@P0: about their information needs with respect to both their
L12@P0: upstream and downstream projects. We investigated what
L13@P0: kind of information is required, why is it necessary, and how
L14@P0: the developers obtain this information.
L15@P0: The results show that the downstream needs are grouped
L16@P0: into three categories roughly corresponding to the different
L17@P0: stages in their relation with an upstream: selection, adoption, and co-evolution. The less numerous upstream needs
L18@P0: are grouped into two categories: project statistics and code
L19@P0: usage.
L20@P0: The current practices part of the study shows that to satisfy many of these needs developers use non-specific tools
L21@P0: and ad hoc methods. We believe that this is a largely unexplored area of research.

L39@P0: 1. INTRODUCTION
L40@P0: Software systems do not exist in a void, but rather in larger
L41@P0: contexts called software ecosystems. Within an ecosystem
L42@P0: a project is surrounded by competing and complementary
L43@P0: projects, and developers interact with fellow developers inside and outside of their own projects, borrowing ideas, idioms, code snippets and sometimes even entire code bases.
L44@P0: In this paper, we identify and categorize the information
L45@P0: needs of developers by analyzing the results of several interviews we conducted with developers that work in an ecosystem context.
L46@P0: Authors define the term “software ecosystem” in different ways [1, 2]. In our work we rely on the definition of
L47@P0: Lungu [3]: “A software ecosystem is a collection of software
L48@P0: projects which are developed and which co-evolve together
L49@P0: in the same environment.” Our definition focuses on the
L50@P0: co-evolution and source-code level inter-dependency aspect
L51@P0: rather than the business aspect.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in the Community version?

The Community version includes all core features for local testing, evaluation, and vulnerability scanning.

What is a probe?

A probe is a single request made to your target system during red team testing.

Why are there usage limits on red teaming?

Certain red teaming plugins require inference for dynamic test generation and grading. The open-source Community version includes up to 10k probes per month at no charge. Enterprise customers can purchase additional probes for larger scale testing.

Who needs the Enterprise version?

Larger teams and organizations that want to continuously monitor risk in development and production.

How does Enterprise pricing work?

Enterprise pricing is customized based on your team's size and needs. Contact us for a personalized quote.

Can I upgrade from Community to Enterprise?

Yes, you can easily upgrade to Enterprise at any time to access additional features and support.

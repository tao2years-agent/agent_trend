Pro
Pro is Devin’s entry-level individual plan. It’s designed for a single developer who uses Devin regularly. Pro includes:
A daily and weekly usage quota that covers Devin sessions,
Devin CLI
, and
Devin Desktop
Pay-as-you-go
on-demand credits
for usage past your quota
Slack, Linear, and
MCP

Pro is a single-user plan. Pro subscribers cannot invite additional members to their organization. To work with teammates on a shared subscription, use the
Teams
plan.
Max
Max is for individual users who consistently exceed the Pro quota. It includes everything in Pro, plus a significantly larger weekly usage quota (with no daily cap), also shared between Devin sessions,
Devin CLI
, and Devin Desktop.
Like Pro, Max is a single-user plan and does not support multiple members.
Teams
The Teams plan is Devin’s self-serve plan for teams working with Devin together. Key properties:
$80/month
: every Teams subscription is a flat $80/month, charged as prepaid
on-demand credits
.
On-demand credits
are shared across the whole team. Every member draws from the same pool, and admins can
top up
at any time.
Every member gets the same access to Devin cloud sessions,
Devin CLI
, and Devin Desktop.
SAML or OIDC single sign-on
for your team account. See
SSO for Teams
.
Cost controls with 3 usage tiers
to cap how much each member can use. See
Cost controls
.
Free mode
: each member can run up to 2 cloud sessions at a time at no cost. See
Free mode
.
Larger teams that need more controls should
contact sales
about Enterprise.
SSO
Teams admins can connect a SAML 2.0 or OIDC identity provider (Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, or any compliant IdP) from
Settings > Authentication
. On a Teams account, SSO signs in existing members only, so invite people before they sign in through SSO. Follow the identity provider setup steps in the
SSO onboarding guide
; its auto-membership, role assignment, and SCIM provisioning steps apply to
Enterprise
only.
Cost controls
Teams admins can cap each member’s usage with up to
3 usage tiers
from
Settings > Usage policies
. Each tier sets a usage limit, members are assigned to a tier or fall back to the default tier, and new work is blocked once a member reaches their limit. The
usage tiers
section of the Usage policies guide explains how tiers work; budgets and budget-scoped limits apply to Enterprise only.
Free mode
On the Teams plan, every member can pick
Free (powered by SWE-2)
in the agent selector to run Devin cloud sessions at no cost. Each member can run up to
2 Free sessions at a time
; to start a third, wait for one to finish or pick another mode. Sessions in other modes are billed as usual.
How quotas work
Pro and Max include a usage allowance that refreshes automatically on a calendar basis:
Pro
has a
daily and weekly
allowance. The daily allowance is more than 1/7 of the weekly, so you can keep working through weekends without giving up overall capacity for the week.
Max
has a
weekly
allowance only, with no daily cap.
Teams
has no per-member allowance: all usage draws from the team’s shared
on-demand credits
.
When you’ve used up your allowance,
on-demand credits
keep you working without interruption. If your on-demand balance is also empty, sessions pause until your allowance refreshes or you purchase more credits.
If you have an eligible ChatGPT Go, Plus, or Pro subscription, you can also
use your ChatGPT plan
to pay for GPT model usage so it doesn’t count against your Devin allowance.
On-demand credits
On-demand credits are prepaid usage credit that fund any work past your plan’s included quota:
Roll over
month-to-month. Purchased credits never expire.
Can be topped up at any time: select
Purchase on-demand usage
on the current plan card in
Settings > Plans
or
Settings > Usage & limits
. Select
Configure auto-reload settings
next to it to refill credits automatically.
Admins can set auto-reload thresholds and default session spending limits from
Settings > Usage & limits
.
On the
Teams
plan, credits are
shared across all members
, with no per-member balance. Any teammate can draw from the shared pool.
On the
Teams
plan, credits fund all usage, and the $80/month subscription is added to the pool as credits each month.
Devin Review and Automations Pricing
Automations
and
Devin Review
are available on self-serve plans.
Teams use shared on-demand credits.
On the Teams plan, Automations and Devin Review draw directly from the team’s shared
on-demand credit
pool.
What happens when you run out of credits.
If you run out of credits, Automations stop running and Devin Review switches to its diff viewer. Top up
on-demand credits
to start Automations again and re-enable AI-powered review.
Public PRs are free.
Anyone can review a public GitHub PR at
devinreview.com
— or by replacing
github.com
with
devinreview.com
in any PR URL — without a Devin account, and no on-demand credits are consumed.
Review managers can keep usage predictable with auto-review rules in
Settings > Review
. Choose when a rule starts (
PR is created
or
PR is ready
) and its cadence (
Only once
or
Every push
), then narrow its scope with repository or author conditions. See
Trigger Modes
in the Devin Review docs for details.

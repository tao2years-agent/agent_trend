This topic explains LaunchDarkly plans you can subscribe to and summarizes the features available on each. LaunchDarkly offers four tiers to choose from to fit your organization’s size and needs.

Plan features
LaunchDarkly offers Developer, Foundation, and Enterprise plans:
Developer
plans help you get started with feature flags for passion projects. Developer plans are free to use and don’t require a credit card.
Foundation
plans include scalable feature management and Experimentation for growing teams.
Enterprise
plans support advanced software delivery practices at Enterprise scale.
Developer plans
You can subscribe as a developer for free with limited usage of the following features:
service connections
monthly active users (MAU)
Experimentation keys
observability features, including
session replays
errors
logs
traces
.
If you subscribe to a Foundation or Enterprise plan, monthly entitlements are included for each of these.

Data Export events
to Foundation and Enterprise plans as an add-on.

the

adaptive triggers

Free trials
When you sign up for a free trial with LaunchDarkly, your trial includes access to all of the LaunchDarkly Enterprise features and Release Autopilot add-ons.
When your trial ends, your account will automatically convert to a free Developer plan. You can also choose to upgrade to a paid Foundation or Enterprise plan.
Here is how your account features change with each plan type:
If you upgrade to an Enterprise plan with the Release Autopilot add-on, you will retain access to all Release Autopilot features and any Release Autopilot configuration you completed as part of your trial.
If you upgrade to an Enterprise plan without the Release Autopilot add-on, you will retain access to all Enterprise features and any Enterprise configuration you completed as part of your trial, such as setting up
teams
or
roles
. LaunchDarkly retains any account data related to Release Autopilot features, but you cannot interact with it.
Any guarded rollout already configured on a flag will be available to start, and any guarded rollouts still in progress will complete. You will not be able to create new guarded rollouts on new flags.
If you move to a Developer or Foundation plan, LaunchDarkly retains any account data related to Enterprise-only features, but you cannot interact with it.
Any LaunchDarkly members that you assigned a custom or preset role will revert to the base role they had before you assigned the custom or preset role, such as the Reader base role.
The
Relay Proxy in offline mode
will still function, but you will not be able to download new flag rules.
You can also
delete your trial account
, which permanently removes your account data.

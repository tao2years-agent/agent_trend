Budgets.
Organization-level and user-level ACU limits are unifying under
Budgets
. Budgets cap ACU usage with one limit for the account, shared pools for teams or projects, and per-user limits inside each pool.
Tracking ACU consumption
Enterprise customers can track ACU consumption at both the Enterprise and Organization level:
Enterprise admins
view Enterprise ACU consumption at
Settings > Consumption
in Enterprise Settings.
Organization admins
view Organization ACU consumption at
Settings > Consumption Analytics
within Organization Settings.
Any user
can see the ACU cost of a specific session from
Session Insights
.
Setting budgets
Enterprise admins set ACU limits with
Budgets
in
Enterprise Settings
.
Budget limits apply to cloud Devin sessions, Devin Review, Devin Desktop, Windsurf JetBrains, and Devin CLI usage billed to the budget’s organizations, including organizations in its child budgets.
Budget tree with utilization per budget (mock data)
When a budget reaches its limit, new work billed to those organizations is blocked. An enterprise admin can raise the limit or set a temporary limit, or users can wait until usage resets in the next monthly billing window. User limits remain in effect independently.

Each budget can also cap each member’s monthly ACU consumption with usage tiers, IdP group mappings, and per-member overrides. See
Usage policies

Frequently asked questions
When will I be billed?
Enterprise customers are billed for ACUs as stated in their order form.
What are Enterprise ACUs?
Enterprise ACUs represent the work performed by Devin for customers on the Enterprise plan, which adheres more strictly to task planning and end-to-end testing. They are distinct from self-serve quota and on-demand credits, and are priced per the customer’s Enterprise order form.
How can I tell which Organization or user consumed the most ACUs?
Enterprise admins can break consumption down by Organization from the
Consumption
page in Enterprise Settings. Org admins can break it down by user from
Consumption Analytics
inside Organization Settings.

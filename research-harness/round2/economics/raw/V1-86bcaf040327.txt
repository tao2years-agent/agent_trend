Devin has two pricing models:
Self-serve
: Free, Pro, Max, and Teams plans you can sign up for directly at
app.devin.ai
. Self-serve usage is billed through a mix of included quota and on-demand credits. See
Self-serve plans
for full details.
Enterprise
: Devin Enterprise customers are billed in Agent Compute Units (ACUs) at the rate set in their order form. See
Enterprise
for how ACU consumption is tracked, or
contact sales
for pricing.
For how Devin meters work in general (how usage accrues, idle/sleep behavior, and tips for keeping consumption under control), see
Usage
. The tips on that page apply to both pricing models.

L41: 
L42: Added in 【22†v0.0.224†github.com】 · 【23†Related issues†github.com】 · 【24†View source†github.com】
L43:

L48: ## 【26†What it does】
L49: 
L50: Checks for `str.format` calls that can be replaced with f-strings.
L51: 
L52: ## 【27†Why is this bad?】
L53: 
L54: f-strings are more readable and generally preferred over `str.format` calls.
L55: 
L56: ## 【28†Example】
L57: 
L58:     `"{}".format(foo)
L59:     `
L60: 
L61: Use instead:
L62: 
L63:     `f"{foo}"
L64:     `

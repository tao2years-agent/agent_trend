Guarded rollouts improvements
You can now run health checks as part of the guarded rollout setup to ensure your flags and metrics are correctly evaluated and emitted before initializing the rollout. This ensures that all necessary data is in place to guard your release.
Additionally, you can now customize rollout duration, giving you greater control over the timing of your releases.

Investigate, page, and catch up with @Rootly in Microsoft Teams.
On this page
Rootly AI is now in Microsoft Teams. Mention @Rootly in an incident channel, a team channel, or a chat, ask in plain language, and it answers right in the conversation. It’s the same agent your Slack teams already use, with the same tools, permissions, and confirmation cards, so responders on Teams can work the whole incident from the place they already are.
Declare an incident and page a teammate from the same Teams thread. Rootly AI confirms before it pages.
Support the investigation
Ask @Rootly what’s going on and it pulls the incident’s context together for you. Connect your observability, code, and docs sources, and Rootly AI investigates with them: ask “has anything changed on checkout-api?” to see recent deploys and feature flag changes next to the incident. It can also point you to related past incidents, so you’re not starting from zero.
Page and manage on-call
Find out who’s on call, check an escalation chain, or page a team without opening another tool. In Teams, you can page and create schedule overrides from incident channels, team channels, and chats with Rootly.
“Who’s on call for payments?”
“Page the SRE escalation policy”
“Create an override so Sam covers my shift on Friday from 9 AM to 5 PM”
Rootly AI asks for confirmation before destructive actions and whenever it needs your sign-off.
Catch up on the bridge
Joining late? Ask “Catch me up” for a short brief on the problem, current status, working theory, open action items, and who’s involved. Rootly AI reads the channel conversation and bridge call transcripts when they’re available, so “What’s happened in the last hour?” gets you current without scrolling or asking someone to recap.
What’s included
@Rootly in Teams incident channels, team channels, and chats
Incident updates from the incident channel: severity, status, roles, and action items
Declare new incidents from any team channel
Paging, on-call lookups, and schedule overrides from channels and chats
Catch-up briefs that draw on the channel and bridge call transcripts
Deploy and change lookups through your connected sources
The same permissions and confirmation cards as Rootly AI in Slack
Why it matters
Every time a responder leaves the incident to check a dashboard, look up a schedule, or ask what they missed, they lose focus and the team loses time. Teams-first organizations can now get the same AI help Slack teams have had, inside the tool they live in.
